# PhishTrace

PhishTrace is a phishing email investigation and incident response platform designed to simulate a real-world SOC investigation workflow.

## Project Goal

The platform investigates a suspicious phishing email from initial triage through threat intelligence enrichment, campaign scoping, account compromise investigation, containment, and incident reporting.

## Investigation Workflow

1. Triage the reported email
2. Analyse email headers and authentication
3. Investigate URLs, domains and infrastructure
4. Enrich indicators using threat intelligence
5. Scope the phishing campaign
6. Investigate suspicious account activity
7. Correlate evidence and assess risk
8. Simulate containment and remediation
9. Map findings to MITRE ATT&CK
10. Generate an incident report
